Quantum Magnifier

Point it at something ordinary. The instrument keeps magnifying past the point where there is anything left to see, and draws what is there instead: photons, bonds, probability, colour charge, and finally the vacuum the whole thing is standing on.

It needs your camera to read the scene. Nothing leaves this device — the video never goes anywhere, and the whole instrument runs offline in this page. Without a camera it falls back to a simulated specimen.

What is being measured, and what is a convention

A camera measures light, not matter. Everything derived from light here is a real calculation on real data; everything about composition is a stated convention, applied consistently so that the same object always reads the same way.

Measured, then computed

  • Luminance → irradiance, anchored to 1 W/m² for a lit scene, giving photon flux, field amplitude √(2I/cε0) and energy density I/c.
  • Hue → dominant wavelength, and from it photon energy hc/λ and the electron de Broglie length h/√(2mE).
  • Luminance gradient → field direction. The field lines over the scene are streamlines of that gradient.
  • Frame-to-frame change → agitation, driving thermal amplitude and the decoherence term.
  • Magnification → Δx, the real size of one screen pixel, which sets the momentum bound Δp ≥ ℏ/2Δx.

Drawn to scale

  • Orbital clouds are sampled from hydrogenic |ψ|² — Γ(3, a0/2) for 1s, Γ(5, a0) for 2p, Γ(7, 3a0/2) for 3d — scaled by the Slater effective charge. Each dot is one possible measurement, not a particle.
  • Nuclear radius is 1.2 fm × A1/3, nucleons uniform in the ball. The proton is drawn at its 0.84 fm charge radius.
  • Bond lengths, Bohr radii and nucleon sizes are all rendered at the dial's current metres-per-pixel, so the jumps between rungs are the real ones.

Convention, not measurement

  • Colour → element. Red reads as oxygen, blue as nitrogen, green and grey as carbon, white as hydrogen, yellow as sulfur, orange as phosphorus, violet as iron. A camera cannot see elements; this is a mapping.
  • Pixel RGB → colour charge. At the partonic rung the three channels weight the three colour charges. The pun is deliberate; the physics of confinement drawn around it is not.
  • Time. Light crosses a micrometre in femtoseconds. The display clock is slowed by the factor shown in the readout so that transport is visible at all.
